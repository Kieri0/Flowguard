Background and objective

Why study DDoS flows?

DDoS traffic can disrupt access to a network service. Traditional intrusion detection can use known signatures; this study examines whether learned flow patterns can help classify records as BENIGN or DDoS. Machine-learning results still depend on the data and evaluation setting. NIST intrusion detection guide · Sommer and Paxson

General objective

Develop and evaluate a network-flow analyzer that classifies records as BENIGN or DDoS using Logistic Regression and Random Forest. The notebook and this site use the same 69 numerical features from the CICIDS2017 Friday DDoS capture. The site does not monitor or block live traffic.
